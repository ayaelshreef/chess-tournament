Tournament rules

Each group plays four Swiss-style rounds. Round 1 is a random draw. Later pairings favor similar scores and balanced colors while avoiding repeat opponents where possible.

A win earns 1 point, a draw ½, and a loss 0. A bye earns 1 point and adds no opponent to the tie-breaks.

Buchholz
Sum of opponents’ scores, including any points they earned from byes.
Sonneborn–Berger
Sum of defeated opponents’ scores and half of drawn opponents’ scores.
Wins
Count of games won. Bye points contribute to the score only.

If all three tie-breaks leave first place tied, record the group playoff winner. The two group winners meet in the championship final.
